AI & Prompting Line

Use AI tools confidently at work and in study — no math degree required.

🚉 6 stations⏱ ~24 hours total💸 All resources free🎯 Students, office professionals, creators and career-switchers.

Artificial intelligence moved from research labs into everyday software in just a few years. Employers no longer ask whether you have heard of AI; they ask whether you can use it to work faster without making costly mistakes. This line gets you there in about 24 focused hours.

You will start with intuition — what a model actually does when it "predicts the next word" — then move to practical prompting, evaluating output, and building small automations. The final station introduces the core machine-learning ideas so you can keep going toward technical roles if you want to.

Destination: A personal prompt library, three automated workflows and the vocabulary to talk about AI with technical teams.

The route

  1. 1What AI really is (and isn't)~3 h

    Learn the difference between traditional software, machine learning and large language models. Understand training data, why models hallucinate, and why confident output is not the same as correct output.

    Elements of AI (free course) ↗3Blue1Brown: Neural networks ↗

  2. 2Prompting fundamentals~4 h

    Practise the five-part prompt: role, task, context, constraints and output format. Rewrite one vague prompt five times and compare the answers side by side to see what each part changes.

    Google Prompting Essentials overview ↗Learn Prompting (open guide) ↗

  3. 3Checking AI output~3 h

    Build a verification habit: ask for sources, cross-check numbers, test code before trusting it, and never paste private or client data into public tools. Create a one-page checklist you will reuse.

    NIST AI Risk Management Framework ↗

  4. 4AI for writing, research & study~4 h

    Use AI as a tutor rather than a ghost-writer: generate practice questions, explain a concept three ways, summarise a reading and then quiz yourself without the summary.

    Our guide: Learning with AIKhan Academy: AI for education ↗

  5. 5No-code automations~5 h

    Connect a form, a spreadsheet and an AI step to remove one repetitive task from your week. Document the before-and-after time saved — it is the most persuasive line on a CV.

    Microsoft Learn: AI skills ↗Google Apps Script basics ↗

  6. 6Machine-learning foundations~5 h

    Meet supervised learning, training vs. test data, overfitting and evaluation metrics through hands-on notebooks. This is your on-ramp if you want to become technical.

    Google ML Crash Course ↗Kaggle Learn: Intro to ML ↗

FAQ

Do I need coding for this line?

No. Stations 1–5 are code-free. Station 6 uses simple Python notebooks that run in the browser.

Which AI tool should I use?

Any mainstream assistant works. The skills — clear prompts and careful verification — transfer between tools.
