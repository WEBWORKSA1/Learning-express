Personal Finance & Investing Line

Budget, borrow, save and invest with clear rules of thumb.

🚉 6 stations⏱ ~18 hours total💸 All resources free🎯 Students, first-job earners, families and anyone starting over financially.

Money skills compound. A few hours learning how interest, taxes and diversification work can change decades of outcomes — yet most people were never taught. This line covers the essentials in plain language.

It is educational, not personalised advice. Rules and products differ by country, so each station points to official consumer-protection resources you can trust.

Destination: A working budget, a debt-payoff plan and a written investing policy you understand.

0 stations · 0%

The route

  1. 1Budgeting that sticks~3 h

    Track one month of spending, then build a simple needs-wants-goals budget. Automate savings on payday.

    CFPB consumer tools ↗Khan Academy Personal Finance ↗

  2. 2Debt & credit~3 h

    How interest really accrues, avalanche vs. snowball payoff, and what moves a credit score.

    CFPB: Credit reports and scores ↗

  3. 3Emergency fund & insurance~2 h

    Size an emergency fund, understand deductibles and premiums, and protect against the risks that cause most financial setbacks.

    Investor.gov: Saving and investing ↗

  4. 4How investing works~4 h

    Stocks, bonds, funds, fees and diversification. Why low-cost broad index funds are a common starting point, and what risk really means.

    Investor.gov basics ↗Bogleheads wiki ↗

  5. 5Tax-advantaged accounts~3 h

    Learn the purpose of retirement and education accounts in your country and how employer matching works.

    Canada.ca: TFSA ↗IRS: Retirement plans ↗

  6. 6Avoiding scams~3 h

    Recognise guaranteed-return pitches, pressure tactics and fake platforms. Learn to verify any adviser or firm before sending money.

    Investor.gov: Protect your investments ↗

FAQ

Stations 1 and 2 — a budget and a payoff plan come before investing.
