Questions, answered

Is Learning-Express really free?

Yes. Every line, guide, tool and quiz is free with no account required. We are supported by advertising, optional donations and partners — never by paywalls.

Do you create the courses?

We design the routes, write the guides and build the tools. The lessons we link to come from reputable open-education providers, universities and creators, credited on every station.

How does Program Match work?

You answer seven questions. We suggest program types that fit and, only if you opt in, may introduce you to education partners. You are never obliged to enrol.

Is my progress saved?

Station check-offs, flashcards and streaks are stored privately in your own browser. Clearing browser data resets them.
