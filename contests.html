30-Day Learning Streak Challenge

Learn something every day for 30 days on any Express Line. Keep your Daily Quiz streak alive and log what you studied.

How to enter: Share a screenshot of your 30-day streak plus a short learning log (doc or post link).

Prizes

  • 1st: $100 learning gift card
  • 2nd: $50 learning gift card
  • 3rd: $25 learning gift card
  • All finishers: featured on the Streak Wall

Judging: Completeness of the streak (40%), quality of the learning log (40%), progress shown (20%).

Explain-It-Simply Video Contest

Use the Feynman technique: explain one concept from any Express Line in a video under 3 minutes.

How to enter: Upload to YouTube (public or unlisted) and submit the link.

Prizes

  • Winner: $150 + featured on our Video Express page
  • Runner-up: $50
  • Creator shortlist: paid commission offer

Judging: Clarity (40%), accuracy (30%), creativity (20%), production (10%).
