## Patterns adopted
- **Guided path, not a catalogue** (Odin Project, freeCodeCamp, Codecademy): → 12 Express Lines with 6 stations each.
- **Matching quiz as lead capture** (Varsity Tutors, Codecademy, MasterClass): → 7-step Program Match funnel with lead scoring and explicit partner consent.
- **Free study tools as traffic magnets** (Quizlet, W3Schools, Alison): → 8 browser tools.
- **Daily habit loop** (Duolingo, Kahoot): → Daily Quiz + streak counter.
- **Donations from a free mission** (Khan, MIT OCW, freeCodeCamp, Odin): → tiered support page with allocation transparency.
- **Ads + ad-free upsell** (W3Schools, SparkNotes, Alison): → AdSense Auto Ads on every page.
- **Newsletter/lead magnet** (TED-Ed, Princeton Review popup): → 30-Day Planner exit-intent modal.
- **Daily fresh content** (Class Central news, TED-Ed): → GitHub Action refreshing YouTube lessons + education news.

## Trademark finding
"LearningExpress" / "LearningExpress Library" is an EBSCO-owned test-prep and skill-building brand. The site therefore: always uses the hyphenated "Learning-Express", a distinct transit-map identity, avoids "Library"/"Learning Center" product naming, and carries an explicit non-affiliation disclosure on every page and on `disclaimer.html`.
