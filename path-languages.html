Language Learning Line

Reach conversational level with daily input, spaced review and real speaking.

🚉 6 stations⏱ ~90 hours total💸 All resources free🎯 Travellers, newcomers, heritage speakers and exam candidates.

Languages are learned through massive comprehensible input — listening and reading material you mostly understand — combined with regular speaking practice. Apps are a good start, but they are only one part of the system.

This line gives you a 90-hour framework that works for any language: build a core vocabulary, absorb native content, review with spaced repetition and speak early, even imperfectly.

Destination: 1,000+ core words, a daily listening habit and your first 10 real conversations.

0 stations · 0%

The route

  1. 1Sounds & survival phrases~8 h

    Learn the sound system and 100 phrases you will use every day. Record yourself and compare with native audio.

    Forvo pronunciation dictionary ↗Language Transfer (free audio) ↗

  2. 2Core vocabulary with spaced repetition~20 h

    Learn the 1,000 most frequent words using flashcards that schedule reviews right before you would forget.

    Anki flashcards ↗Our Flashcard tool

  3. 3Daily app practice~15 h

    Use one app for 15 minutes daily to keep momentum — treat it as warm-up, not the whole workout.

    Duolingo ↗Memrise ↗

  4. 4Comprehensible input~25 h

    Watch beginner-friendly video and graded readers. Aim for content where you understand at least 70%.

    YouTube: comprehensible input ↗

  5. 5Grammar as a map~10 h

    Study grammar to understand patterns you have already heard, not as a list of rules to memorise first.

    OpenStax / open textbooks ↗

  6. 6Speaking & feedback~12 h

    Book conversation exchanges or tutors. Keep a mistake journal and recycle errors into flashcards.

    Tandem language exchange ↗italki tutors ↗

FAQ

How long to become conversational?

It depends heavily on the language and your native tongue, but many learners reach basic conversations after 100–200 hours of mixed practice.

Can I learn two at once?

It is possible but slower. Build one to a comfortable level first.
