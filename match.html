Program Match FAQ

Why is Program Match free?

Some education partners pay a referral fee when a learner who opted in chooses to contact or enrol with them. This never changes the free resources we recommend.

Will I get spam calls?

No. Leave the partner box unticked and you'll hear only from us. If you tick it, at most three relevant partners may reach out, and you can opt out anytime.

Do you cover Canada, the UK and India?

Yes. Online program options are global; we tailor suggestions to your country.
