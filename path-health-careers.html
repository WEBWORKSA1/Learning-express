Health & Care Careers Line

Explore healthcare roles and build the science foundation they require.

🚉 6 stations⏱ ~40 hours total💸 All resources free🎯 Career-switchers, high-school students and caregivers considering formal training.

Healthcare offers some of the most stable and meaningful careers available, from personal support work and pharmacy technicians to nursing and medical imaging. Each path has different entry requirements, timelines and costs.

This line helps you compare roles with official labour data, build the anatomy and terminology foundation most programs expect, and prepare to choose an accredited program. It is career education, not clinical advice.

Destination: A shortlist of suitable roles, foundation knowledge and a program-selection checklist.

FAQ

Can I train fully online?

Theory often can be online, but most licensed roles require supervised clinical hours in person.

Which roles have the shortest training?

Support and technician roles often take months rather than years — compare them in Station 1.
