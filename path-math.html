MATH

Math Foundations Line

Arithmetic to algebra and statistics — rebuild confidence step by step.

🚉 6 stations⏱ ~80 hours total💸 All resources free🎯 Returning students, college-placement candidates, parents and tradespeople.

Many adults believe they are "not a math person" when they simply missed a foundation years ago. Mathematics is cumulative: fix the missing layer and everything above it becomes easier.

This line uses free mastery-based practice so you can find your real starting point, close gaps and progress to the algebra and statistics needed for college, trades and technical careers.

Destination: Algebra fluency, statistics basics and the ability to tackle quantitative courses.

0 stations · 0%

The route

  1. 1Find your level~2 h

    Take a diagnostic and start one level below where you feel comfortable.

    Khan Academy Math ↗

  2. 2Numbers, fractions & percent~15 h

    Fractions, decimals, ratios and percentages — the everyday math behind finance, recipes and measurements.

    Khan Academy Arithmetic ↗Math is Fun ↗

  3. 3Pre-algebra~15 h

    Negative numbers, order of operations, exponents and simple equations.

    Khan Academy Pre-algebra ↗

  4. 4Algebra I~25 h

    Linear equations, inequalities, functions and graphs. Use a graphing calculator to see what equations mean.

    Khan Academy Algebra 1 ↗Desmos graphing calculator ↗

  5. 5Geometry & measurement~10 h

    Area, volume, angles, the Pythagorean theorem and unit conversion.

    Khan Academy Geometry ↗

  6. 6Statistics & probability~13 h

    Data displays, averages, spread and basic probability.

    OpenStax Introductory Statistics ↗Paul's Online Math Notes ↗
